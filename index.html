I hold an M.Sc. in Structural Engineering from Ferdowsi University of Mashhad, Iran. My work focuses on data-driven methods for understanding and monitoring civil infrastructure — structural health monitoring, vibration-based dynamics, and machine learning for damage detection — with the aim of catching structural problems earlier and more reliably than manual inspection allows.

SHEET 01About

About

My recent work explores unsupervised and deep-learning approaches for early damage detection in bridges under environmental and operational variability — conditions like temperature swings and traffic load that can mask real structural damage in sensor data if not accounted for.

I'm currently based in Christchurch, New Zealand, and am looking to continue this research through further study, with particular interest in PhD opportunities in structural health monitoring and AI-enabled infrastructure.
