01 / ABOUT

Bridging research and
real-world engineering.

My background brings together structural engineering, applied machine learning, and construction delivery. I hold an M.Sc. in Structural Engineering from Ferdowsi University of Mashhad, where my thesis focused on machine-learning-based compressive strength modelling of steel fibre-reinforced concrete.

My research focuses on vibration-based structural health monitoring, bridge damage detection, and concrete technology. My professional practice covers structural analysis, quantity surveying, contractor payment assessments and construction progress reporting.

Based in Christchurch, New Zealand, I welcome collaborations and opportunities in structural engineering and doctoral research.
