About

I am a structural engineer and independent researcher based in Christchurch, New Zealand. I hold an M.Sc. in Structural Engineering from Ferdowsi University of Mashhad, where my research included machine learning for concrete materials and structural engineering.

My current research direction is focused on Structural Health Monitoring (SHM), especially data-driven and unsupervised learning methods for early damage detection in civil infrastructure. I am interested in developing practical, interpretable, and robust AI methods that can operate under real-world environmental and operational variability.
