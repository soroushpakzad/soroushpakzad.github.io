About

I hold an M.Sc. in Structural Engineering from Ferdowsi University of Mashhad, Iran. My research focuses on data-driven methods for understanding and monitoring civil infrastructure, with particular interests in Structural Health Monitoring (SHM), structural dynamics and vibration, artificial intelligence, and damage detection.

My recent work has explored unsupervised and deep-learning approaches for early damage detection in bridges under environmental and operational variability. I am currently based in Christchurch, New Zealand, and am interested in pursuing further research in structural engineering and AI-enabled infrastructure.
